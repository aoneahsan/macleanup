# Social post cards — overview

## Purpose

Makes every image on the owner's LinkedIn feed look custom-designed for its product and its post: the product's own
palette, type, mark and illustrations around real screens, with a clear in-image CTA. It exists because the shared
June card template read as AI-made; the owner had every one of those cards deleted on 2026-11-02.

## What this skill does

- Builds a product brief from the product's repo and live app.
- Designs one card per post (10 per series), seven single images and three PDF carousels, each with a different composition.
- Captures fresh real screens with the automation browser.
- Exports SVG masters to PNG and PDF with documented commands, and records everything in `assets/CARDS.md`.
- Reviews each card against the anti-AI checklist and the rubric before it is queued.

## When it gets invoked

Writing or reviewing images for a `linkedin-social-content/<slug>/` series, or any social post image for a product.
